ABSTRACT
Communication is the basis of human identity and the fundamental basis of connecting one person with another person. Although having a healthy relationship with the opposite sex has physical, psychological, and social effects and benefits, it sometimes leads to abusive relationships and debilitating symptoms. Emotional communication described as abusing in an intimate relationship is the most painful form of violence. One of the main challenges of mental health systems is the harmful effects of this type of relationship on people, which causes hurts that naturally take more time to heal and affect a significant part of the world’s population. Since knowing the consequences and factors affecting emotional relationships will lead to the improvement of methods of prevention and dealing with the harms caused by mental health guardians, this protocol is designed to identify the consequences and factors affecting emotional relationships. Identifying these factors can be used as a guide for decision-makers in the mental health system in preventive and therapeutic interventions.

BODY
Introduction

The ability to create and maintain a sincere and satisfying relationship is one of the most important developmental tasks and predicts the psychological well-being of an individual.[1] A strong intimate relationship affects a person’s psychological well-being by facilitating the satisfaction of their needs for love, support, affection, and understanding, and provides a platform where a person can easily achieve their personal goals and enjoy other areas of their lives with satisfaction.[2]

These relationships form an important part of most young people’s lives and can be important aspects of identity, a source of intimacy, emotional security, and social status for them. When these relationships are damaged, and the person is exposed to abuse, it may be harmful and emotionally uncomfortable and associated with stress[3] and negative emotions such as anger, anxiety, and jealousy.[4] In fact, emotional abuse, which is also known as emotional torment, and its long-term consequences are completely negative and lead to physical and mental disorders, can be seen in the interpersonal relationships of young people.[5] Emotional abuse is nonphysical behavior used to control, threaten, dominate, humiliate, punish, or isolate another person through fear or shame. Behaviors caused by psychological abuse include language damage (insults, humiliation, blame, and continuous criticism) to more subtle tactics such as intimidation, jealousy, denial, games, and dissatisfaction.[6]

Unlike physical violence, which is clearly visible, detecting and reporting emotional abuse is a challenge, and this causes it to be hidden in relationships and communication, so little attention is paid to it.[7] With the increasing number of emotional relationships, abusing relationships have also increased, and girls who are in this type of relationship are often not aware of its signs and consequences. This phenomenon is not limited to a particular society or class and is seen in all cultures and societies of the world as an individual, family, and social problem.[6] Research has shown that prevalence of emotional abuse is relatively high and in some societies, this issue is recognized as a serious issue.[5]

Research shows that one of every ten students has experienced emotional abuse in the last one year at Midwestern University[5]; Also, dominance/intimidation and hostile withdrawal are forms of emotional abuse that are related to suicidal thoughts.[89] In another research, it has been shown that the experiences of emotional abuse are accompanied by various tactics such as devaluing,[10]questioning someone, deliberately forgetting and controlling conversations, and with adverse consequences of mental and physical health and dissatisfaction[11] It is also associated with a decrease in self-confidence,[1213]mistrust of others, and in some cases with post-traumatic growth.[13] Also, a study in Iran showed that emotional abuse predicts mental disorders such as depression, anxiety, fear, paranoid thoughts, and obsessions in women.[14]

Considering that knowledge and awareness of the influencing factors and the consequences of abusing emotional relationships can help prevent and treat the damage caused, it is very important to identify the influencing factors in this type of relationship. Therefore, identifying these factors and their consequences can be the first step to develop a suitable counseling and treatment strategy that can be used at the level of educational, academic, counseling centers, and even the policy-making of the country’s mental health system. These measures can help improve mental health at the community level.

By reviewing the literature, it seems that until now, a comprehensive review study has not been performed regarding the influencing factors and the consequences of abusing emotional relationships. Therefore, the main goal of this study is to identify the influencing factors and the consequences of emotionally abusing relationships. This protocol is presented with the aim of informing other researchers about the continuation of this research to avoid doing similar and repetitive work.

Materials and Methods

This review study includes quantitative and qualitative studies to identify the consequences and factors affecting emotional relationships of unmarried girls.

And the retrieved information will be used in the form of a scope review, and no critical evaluation of the studies will be performed, and the number, scope, and nature of existing studies related to abusing emotional relationships will be reviewed. Finally, the list of consequences and factors affecting emotional relationships will be extracted.

Inclusion criteria

This research examines all studies with a quantitative, qualitative, mixed design, original texts, and theses published in English and Farsi in the time range of 1990 to 2023.

Exclusion criteria

Studies before 1990 in languages other than Farsi and English, published reports, and studies that there is no access to their full text or are outside the field of abusing emotional relationships, will not be included in this study.

Search strategy

Keywords

The search terms used to identify related studies in the searched databases include full Farsi or English synonyms that are used for the effects and factors affecting abusing emotional relationships.

These keywords are:

“Emotional abuse,” “Psychological abuse,” “Dating relationship,” “Dating relationship abuse,” “Emotional abuse relationship*,” “Emotional partner* abuse,” “romantic Partner* abuse,” “Emotional blackmail,” “Emotionally abusive relationship*,” “toxic relationship*,” “intimate partner relationship*” Girls, “college students” “Effective factor*”“context factor*,” Background, Outcome*,

Databases

To increase the comprehensiveness of the review, multiple sources are searched. In this regard, the databases for retrieving texts in English including (WoS), (Scopus), (PubMed), and Google scholar and for retrieving texts in Persian, (Magiran), Scientific Information of Academic Jihad (SID), Research Institute of Sciences and Iran Information Technology (Iran Doc) will be searched.

Therefore, the search strategy will be as follows.

(“Emotional abuse” OR “Psychological abuse” OR “Dating relationship” OR “Dating relationship abuse” OR “Emotional abuse relationship*” OR “Emotional partner* abuse” OR “romantic Partner* abuse” OR “Emotional blackmail” OR “Emotionally abusive relationship*” OR “toxic relationship*” OR “intimate partner relationship*”) AND TITLE-ABS-KEY (“college students” OR girls) AND TITLE-ABS-KEY (“Effective factor*” OR “context factor*” OR background OR outcomes).

Data extraction

After implementing the determined search strategy, abstracts of related documents are extracted under the supervision of the team leader (associate professor of psychology). Then the full text of the identified related studies is extracted and the full text is checked by two reviewers and finally the relevant studies are identified.

This study will be conducted based on the preferred reporting items for reviews [Figure 1].[15] According to this protocol, the following steps including the selection of studies and data extraction are performed by two researchers independently, and if there is a difference in the obtained results, the third researcher is consulted. Finally, all documents entered into the study will be reviewed and approved by the team leader.

Flow diagram showing selection of articles reviewed in accordance with the preferred reporting items.[15]

A data extraction form is designed that specifies what data should be extracted from the document [Appendix 1]. EndNote20 is used to manage extracted sources.

Data analysis

Considering the objectives of the present review, the consequences and factors affecting emotionally abusing relationships will be extracted from the final articles.

Discussion

This review identifies the consequences and factors affecting emotional relationships. The results of this study can be used as a guide for psychologists and counselors at the level of educational, academic, counseling centers, and policy-making areas of the country’s mental health system. It is expected that based on that, we can see an increase in the use of research results in this field.
